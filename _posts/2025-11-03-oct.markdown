He is thankful to God for saving him from the certain death of the sea by sending a fish to swallow him and acknowledges only God can save. My prayer is that we too can continue to be thankful to God for saving each of us by his grace into his family!

        <h3>preaching at SOW</h3>
        For the first time ever I preached two weeks in a row! Captivate is blessed with the opportunity to preach regularly at SOW macquarie, and this semester Radman took the lead in preparing a five series in 2 Timothy, and I was able to preach from 2 Tim 2:14-26, and 2 Tim 3:1-4:5. It was definitely testing to get the exegesis done and preach well two weeks in a row! My main points for each sermon were “Handle the word of truth carefully for your life and for the lives around you.” and “Remain in the word of God while we wait for Christ’s return”. I will endeavour to upload the sermons here soon!

        <h3>reflections</h3>
        <ul class="list-group list-group-flush">
            <li class="list-group-item">- Being organised saves a lot of energy when things are busy—putting in the extra effort to be organised with my tasks early on has been paying off well as I handle lots of different responsibilities, I spend less time having to think about what to work on and it helps me stay grounded as I have a good idea of what I’m doing and how it serves the gospel.</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- Repeating the same task (even if hard) goes a long way for growing! Preaching twice in a row helped me with my confidence and delivery for the second time, likewise with service leading and kid’s church. In all of these things I have definitely felt that God has grown me through persevering in these ministries as he prepares me for a lifetime of ministry!</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- Keep loving the word even when it’s busy. It’s important for me (and for all of us) to spend time in God’s word no matter how busy it is. I’ve felt this in the busy-ness of getting stuff done it’s easy to just try to get work done, but as I preached on 2 Timothy I was challenged to keep letting the word shape me. Handling the word of truth carefully is not only exegeting and preaching well, but also letting that word of truth dwell in my heart and change me.</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- Leading well serves the gospel well! I have definitely benefitted in being led and trained well over many years, and I see this also across many ministry teams at captivate! I am thankful for the team leaders that I meet up with as God has been gracious in equipping these people well to serve him for his glory!</li>
        </ul>
