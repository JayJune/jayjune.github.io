        <h3>reflections</h3>
        <ul class="list-group list-group-flush">
            <li class="list-group-item">- Ministry expands to fill time really easily. There's plenty of work for me to do and I've had to consider my own limitations and how to prioritise tasks and make the best use of my time. I've also had to consider how to be intentional in my time off and how I can be honouring Bec well in that time.</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- It's good to enjoy God's creation as we travel, and it's both glorifying to God and encouraging to other Christians when we travel intentionally keeping Jesus in mind and praying for opportunities to speak of God's word.</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- Relationships and 1 to 1s are super high value! They're a great way to get to know people better and fellowship more personally together. I already knew this but being able to do it purposefully as an apprentice has given me a lot of energy and intentionality to how I meet with other guys. Pray for more opportunities!</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- This is carried over from last month, but it is still an incredible privelege to read God's Word as part of my apprenticeship. I've never been reading so much Bible and Christian literature before, and I am learning a lot as God feeds me from his scriptues. As his word is applicable to all of life, I've found that often a bit of what I'm reading personally suddenly becomes relevant to other tasks and things happening in life!</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
