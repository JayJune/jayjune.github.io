        <h3>staff retreat</h3>
        On an unusually sunny week of August we spent three days together as a staff team, reviewing the year past and planning the year ahead. Last year I couldn’t make it for the whole staff retreat, so I’m thankful that I got to spend this time with the staff team in prayer, reflection, and planning together. Despite being a retreat, we didn’t do much relaxing. Instead I’ve been continually challenged and stretched by what it looks like to be intentional in caring for so many different people lovingly and strategically, and also feeling the pinch of not being able to do enough. There was lots of prayerful and fruitful planning for what the next year looks like for each of us in personal growth, godliness, and ministry areas, as well as what it looks like to keep growing our church in being more like Christ, to be captivated by God’s glory and compelled to live with him for many years to come!

        <h3>preaching from Philippians</h3>
        I’m thankful to God (and the congregation) for the opportunity to keep growing in pastoring people in teaching, especially through preaching. I had the opportunity to preach from Philippians 3:1-11 (you can <a href="https://captivatechurch.org/sermons/phil1-1-11-ndfp4-dtf86-4jcj6-wp685-j6k76-9a7d4">listen to the sermon here</a>), and my big idea was that we can consider everything else worth nothing before Christ who is worth everything. As always, as I’ve been able to spend time going deep in to a passage, I’m struck by God’s wisdom, how he speaks into our lives, how little I know, and how much I need Jesus. This passage in particular, reminded me of how I need to continue to grow in my humility, grow in my love for Jesus, grow in letting him shape how I make decisions in my life, small and big ones, and grow in being joyful in suffering for the gospel. Praise God that every opportunity I get to teach to a wider audience he continues to grow me in being a better communicator, in being sharper with my content and also being more confident and clear in my speaking!

        <h3>persevering in leadership</h3>
        As the end of the year looms ahead I’ve started thinking about what it looks like to finish up well with the teams that I’m leading. As I’ve been doing this, I’ve had to evaluate my role and impact on each of these teams, and it has revealed to me that leading teams takes up a lot of my energy! It’s not what I gravitate to, and sometimes I’ve relied a lot on solid team leaders for things to happen, which have been fruitful, but in doing so I think I haven’t been able to love, care, and encourage them as much as I’d like to. With the breadth of tasks I’ve also fallen short of things that I had planned to do with some teams, and these together have reminded me to first persevere and grow in loving people, and to continue to think intentionally about how to lead teams well for fruitful ministry.

        <h3>youth</h3>
        Youth continues to be a great time! This month one of our youth invited a school friend who doesn’t regularly go to church. He has since been back every week and seems pretty comfortable hanging with the youth. He currently goes to a Catholic school and so has some knowledge about God and the Bible, and was even able to tell our discussion group that all people have sinned! We’re still going through our Biblical theology series, looking at God’s big plan throughout the Bible, which has been helpful for our boys to see (see again, for some of them) how God has been faithful throughout the whole Bible, and it has been good for raising bigger questions about life and what God has made us for.
