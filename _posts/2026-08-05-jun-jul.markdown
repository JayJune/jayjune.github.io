        I’ve been reflecting on creation and the fall as we go through an overview of the Bible at youth, and Psalm 8 has reminded me of how profound God’s love is, how much he loves and cares for us in the very work of creation. In God’s wisdom, he has made all of creation, and he has made us in his image to rule under him. But as we live in a world affected by sin, we know that not all people live acknowledging Christ as Lord over all. Praise God for how he has revealed to us how Jesus rules over all! My prayer is for us to continue to be part of God’s work in pointing people to Christ as Lord over the whole universe.

        <h3>captivate ryde</h3>
        Our church amalgamated with another and is now called Captivate Ryde Presbyterian Church! Praise God for his faithfulness in being at work in so many people coming together. The first Sunday we had as a new congregation went pretty much as smooth as possible. For the first month it was a month of cold Sunday mornings, but I showed up early each week to be on welcoming, and each week was a bit of tweaking and a few changes with settling in. Since amalgamation, our attendance is now hovering around 300 people each week! Praise God for this fruit, including a good number of newcomers and also Non-Christians who have visited during our open house series. Pray that God would help us to continue to love these people well, and see people come to know Jesus more and grow mature in Him.

        <h3>holiday kids club</h3>
        School holidays are a welcome break for me from the routine of scripture and youth each week, but with the winter school holidays comes our holiday kid’s program. This year the theme of our program was pirate mania, and each day we followed the story of three sailors who mutinied and became pirates, only to be kidnapped and caught up with other pirates. The resolution was the very captain they rebelled against, saves them! But in doing so he had to give his own life. Each day’s program also included multiple games, a Bible story, food and craft. My main responsibility was to run the games, which was a good opportunity for me to continue to be stretched in a different range of skills, because designing and leading games doesn’t come naturally to me. Praise God for a successful week where the games were enjoyed, we were able to serve alongside many volunteers from our church, and we were able minister to over 40 kids! Pray for continued opportunities to minister to these kids and these families, some of these kids have a sharp understanding of the gospel but come from non-Christian families and don’t go to church, pray that they would truly believe in Jesus and that their whole families would repent and believe too.

        <h3>youth: so, what’s the plan?</h3>
        Youth has kicked off for term 3, and we’re teaching from the whole Bible this term! Our series called “So, what’s the plan?” traces God’s plan throughout the whole Bible. The preparation leading up to this term has been somewhat challenging as the teaching that I’m usually doing and am comfortable with is usually set within a book of the Bible. We’ve wrestled with teaching faithfully and to the heart, instead of just teaching head knowledge, and teaching a clear and distinct application each week that isn’t immediately "just trust Jesus". In week 2 of term I taught on the fall, and at the end of our night we were thankful as leaders because all of our youth that came had a better understanding of the weight of sin, the effect of it on our relationship with God, and how much it cost for our sins to be paid for.

        <h3>reflections</h3>
        <ul class="list-group list-group-flush">
            <li class="list-group-item">- As I look toward the second half of 2026, I’m trying to take up more opportunities for personal discipleship. I’ve realised that it is not something that plays to my strengths, and as a result I’ve put it lower on my priorities. But, I have enjoyed being able to meet up with people and speak into peoples lives, and seen the fruit of it! As a new dad I’ve also found I’ve been able to speak into people’s lives more readily. I’m thankful that God continues to stretch me and grow me for his work in different ways!</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- I thought I was good at juggling different tasks because my last job I’d work on different projects, but now I realise that even across different projects my tasks were usually somewhat the same… Now doing ministry I’ve realised that working on multiple things at the same time is something that I need to keep growing in. I find it easy to work on something when I have momentum and focus hard on that, but as a result some other things might fall behind until I get time to focus on them individually. In these last two months I’ve found myself with less support as different people on the staff team have taken leave or had their own responsibilities which meant I had more time to work independently. I can’t imagine what it’d be like to be the sole pastor of a church, but I definitely felt the need and the weight of being capable of handling a range of different responsibilities faithfully by myself.</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- I’ve been wrestling with what it looks like to be personally okay with the quality of my work not being “perfect”. I enjoy being in the word and being faithful in my exegesis, but I feel that I’ve often gone beyond what’s required in my reading, and with my current capacity and responsibilities this has been slowing me down. It’s always good to be faithful, but part of me going above and beyond to get it right can sometimes be a fear of people and a point of pride. So as I labour on I continue to wrestle with what it looks like to work faithfully and sustainably, and teach for God’s glory and not my own.</li>
        </ul>
