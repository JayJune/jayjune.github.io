Working with the staff team to not only get ministry done but to plan for years ahead has challenged me to think about how to be a faithful leader who uses the resources which God has provided us with effectively, and I'm excited to think about where God will take captivate over the years and also how God has been growing me for his kingdom.

        <h3>captivate youth</h3>
        Thanks for praying for the youth! We have been going through the book of Galatians, learning about how the gospel is all or nothing—it’s perfect for our faith and salvation, but adding anything legalistic to it would mean no good news at all. The term so far has been a fruitful time, we’ve had a few unexpected visitors who have heard the gospel from the book of Galatians! Praise God for one of the youth, A, who has started coming regularly this term of his own desire, and he continues to reflect on what the Bible means for his life and has a desire to know God better. He even started coming to church and caught public transport by himself to get there! We’ve also started planning for term 4 in the book of Jonah and fitting in some evangelistic opportunities as well as a prayer and praise event to close off the year!

    <div class="col-lg-6 my-4">
        <h3>being a father</h3>
        Thanks for praying for us. Parenthood has been a wild journey, and while I knew that I’d never be fully prepared to be a father God continues to surprise me and teach me new things. I am still learning what it means to be a husband, a father, and a faithful gospel teacher. This past month has been a lot of acclimatising to being busier more than ever before with the least consistent sleep I’ve ever had in my life. I’ve continued to be humbled in this time and reminded of my own limitations and my weaknesses, yet there is an overwhelming joy to see how God has created this beautiful child of ours. As we look ahead we continue to trust in God to grow and sustain her, and ultimately save her for his glory.

        <h3>reflections</h3>
        <ul class="list-group list-group-flush">
            <li class="list-group-item">- I need to continue on working on leading myself to use my time better—to be a better husband, father, and leader. It’s easy when it gets busy to just do what I need to do and get by, but I’ve found it helpful to continue to think hard about setting my sights on who I want to grow to be, why, and how I can be doing that now. As I continue to look to Jesus, God gives me energy to see the sacrifices that are made now are for my growth and ultimately for God’s glory.</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- God has been gracious in gathering a faithful and gifted team here at captivate. It is so easy to work alongside the staff team, the elders, and the lay leaders, because they’re all switched on for the gospel. There’s so much to learn from this bank of wisdom and I need to keep looking to what I can learn in my time as an apprentice here!</li>
            <li class="list-group-item">- God has been teaching us to trust him more. When your sleep is bad and there’s so many things not yet done, I’m reminded of my weak and fragile body and how only God sustains us.</li>
        </ul>
