Thank you for your prayer and partnership! 

Happy new year! I hope you've enjoyed your holidays or whatever you've been up to in January! Leading up to starting in January I was excited to be working at church but also a little nervous knowing I'd have to endure the initial settling in preiod - I've definitely felt the busy-ness this month. Adjusting to new work has been tiring, but it has been rewarding in knowing that it ultimately has eternal value. When I reflect on my previous job, I'm reminded of times when I was not sure what value was being provided in the work which we were doing. But as I've been working with a great team at church, everything has purpose and aligns with God's mission to see the save lost, with the goal of producing gospel fruit.  
Here's what's been happening in my work this month!

I was invited to be at our church's session retreat, and alongside the elders we were able to keep thinking about what church looks like in the next 10 years. A team from Reach Australia came to help us think about strategic decisions and structures which could be helpful in growing our church over the next few years. We reflected on how Captivate has grown in the last 5 years in light of our previous plan for 2025, and planned for what our church might look like in 2030 and 2035! I was really encouraged to see how God has been gracious and faithful in growing our church in Ryde, and it was great to imagine and think purposefully in how we can be part of continuing to grow God's kingdom here in Ryde. I have been especially thankful for the staff team and elders who are a group of faithful men who are on for Jesus! 💪

Although I'm not formally doing MTS, I am supported by the Presbyterian church to do my apprenticeship, and most of these apprentices are doing MTS so they invited the apprentices at Captivate to come! I was encouraged seeing over 100 apprentices from across Australia who were on for the gospel meet together to share in fellowship with one another. As we went through which they  G8 conference and network group

Over the holidays the kid's ministry team is taking a well deserved break, so there isn't a formal program for them to go to but we've been intentional in still trying to engage them. For January our church has been going through the book of Jude and we've also been presenting our passages through a skit appropriate for the kids! I was able to act in one of them and with the help of Rad (the 2nd year apprentice at Captivate) we wrote two of the scripts for the kid's spot. It's a story of how Earth sends one of their travellers to a distant planet called Candytron, where everything is made with Candy, and they come under attack from another planet called Sodius Chloridus, their ruler threatening to turn the whole planet into salt. Each week our main character encourages us from the book of Jude to defend the planet, and in turn defend and contend for the faith which we have in Jesus. Having only briefly done kid's ministry (and not having done it for a long while) I found it challenging but rewarding to write the scripts. To distill the message of the passage into something which could be digested through a narrative of fantasy was hard for me, but it was greate to see children engage with the Bible! I also enjoyed being a little goofy and acting in one week, where I acted as a false prophet encouraging the people of Candytron to accept salt with their sugar! 😱

This year I am joining the youth team, and late last year I was able to join our team in planning for this year. To kick-off the year we were able to meet with the parents to think about what youth looks like this year and how our teaching can be structured around our vision to see the youth live boldly for Christ! I felt encouraged as I met parents who were keen not just to have their kids attend youth group, but to pray with us as we partnered with them in discipling the youth. As I've grown up in a non-Christian household, I've felt more and more the joy and importance of parents being the primary spiritual leader for children, and it was so amazing to see parents who were committed to raising their children faithfully to grow in the wisdom of our Lord. It has been great also to work alongside a faithful team of youth leaders,
